# Traffic Simulation: Intersections vs Roundabouts

A NetLogo project comparing traffic flow at signalized intersections versus roundabouts.

## Two Simulation Models

### Model 1: Three Signalized Intersections
Simulates three traffic-light controlled intersections in a row. Vehicles follow traffic signals with red/green cycles, stop for red lights, and can turn left, right, or go straight. Measures traffic flow efficiency through horizontal vehicle count.

### Model 2: Three Roundabouts
Simulates three roundabouts (traffic circles) in a row. Vehicles yield to traffic already in the roundabout, follow right-of-way rules, and navigate circular paths. No traffic lights - flow is controlled by natural gaps and yielding.

## Comparison
The project allows comparing:
- Traffic flow efficiency (cars passed over time)
- Queue formation and waiting times
- Traffic behavior at different control systems
- Gridlock prevention mechanisms

## How to Use
1. Open either `.nlogo` file in NetLogo
2. Click "Setup" to initialize
3. Click "Go" to run simulation
4. Observe vehicle movement and performance metrics

## Goal
Compare which system (traffic lights or roundabouts) provides better traffic flow under similar conditions.
